# 2027: Research Direction

triNetra's ongoing research programme is informed by findings from the Eagle Insight Platform assessments conducted while it was an active offering, and from Framework Building engagements now underway. Specific research directions depend on what those findings reveal about structural assessment requirements the Eagle Framework does not yet fully address.

Confirmed research directions:

**Biotech Insight Platform development.** The Biotech Domain study currently in progress is the research foundation for a Biotech Insight Platform. If the study's findings confirm Eagle Framework applicability to the biotech domain, platform development begins.

**Domain manifest library expansion.** The current 30-domain manifest library will be extended based on demand. New domains are added when sufficient evidence exists from real engagements to calibrate the domain-specific parameters.

**Cross-platform methodology refinement.** Findings from Eagle Insight Platform assessments and Framework Building engagements are used to refine the Eagle Framework dimensions for the research-phase platforms. Epistemic priors, utility bounds, and weighting parameters are adapted for energy, manufacturing, infrastructure, and defence domain characteristics.

---

## Ecosystem Platforms

The research-phase platforms (Energy, Manufacturing, Infrastructure, Defence) continue domain research. The first post-Eagle platform launch date will be announced based on research maturity, not a fixed schedule.

The criterion for launch is research readiness: the domain manifest library must be complete for at least ten canonical domain configurations, and the scoring architecture must be calibrated using domain-specific test cases.
